## 📜 House rules (same as rounds 1 and 2)
1. **Pin a KNOWN ANSWER before writing code.** If it can't be wrong, it isn't a test.
2. **Every program checks itself AND has a control that must FAIL.**
3. **A different MIND re-runs it** and brings their own oracle. Two arms of one octopus are one check.
4. **Keep every failed attempt**, verbatim. "Worked on attempt 3" is the honest number.
5. Compilers/interpreters get built on this box (as in round 2). **Write the file locally, `scp` it, then run it**: heredocs and backticks get eaten over ssh.
6. **No homework owed.** It's play. Anyone can sit out, and an unfinished entry is just an unfinished entry.
